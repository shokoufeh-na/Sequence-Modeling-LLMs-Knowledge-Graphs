# Sequence-Modeling-LLMs-Knowledge-Graphs
In Assignment 3, you analyzed financial news headlines and investigated their potential impact on stock prices.
In this assignment, you will extend that work by exploring how different natural language processing (NLP)
techniques and external knowledge can improve financial news analysis.
You will work with sequence models, Large Language Models (LLMs), Retrieval-Augmented Generation
(RAG), and knowledge graphs. Specifically, you will perform:
1. Sequence Modeling: Develop and evaluate LSTM- and Transformer-based models to classify financial
news according to its market impact.
2. LLMs and RAG: Use an LLM to classify financial news, and investigate whether retrieving relevant
news articles improves classification performance.
3. Knowledge Graphs and GraphRAG: Construct a small, domain-specific financial knowledge graph
and use graph-based retrieval to answer questions about relationships among companies, products,
events, and market impacts.
The overall goal is to understand the strengths and limitations of these approaches and investigate how different
forms of information representation and retrieval can support financial news analysis.
